FIRST-USE SAFETY ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Professional Diagnostic & Tuning Tool

CarbTune provides diagnostic and calibration guidance from the vehicle information, measurements and observations supplied to it. Vehicle condition and component configuration must still be verified by the technician.

CarbTune guidance does not replace appropriate mechanical inspection, testing, safe work practices or technician judgment. Safety hard-stops remain active at every guidance level.
